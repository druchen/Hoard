# Hoard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Hoard is a Chrome extension that saves images you choose to folders on your own computer.

## What Hoard does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit personal data to the developer or to anyone else.
- It has no accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell or share any data.

## What happens when you save an image

- Only when you start a save (right-click an image and choose Hoard, or press H), Hoard reads that image's address and, on some sites, its title to name the file.
- The image is downloaded by your browser from the site it came from, using your existing browser session, and converted in your browser to the format you chose.
- The image is then written to the folder you picked by the Hoard native bridge, a small program you install on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Settings

Your save folders (names, paths and shortcut keys), image format, quality and notification preferences are stored locally in your browser's extension storage. They are not synced or transmitted.

## Permissions

Hoard asks for access to all websites so it can download the image you choose from whichever site or image server hosts it. It does not read or change page content beyond what is described above.
